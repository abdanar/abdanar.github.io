Let $A$ be an $n\times n$ positive semidefinite matrix. Then

$$
\operatorname{per}(A)^2
\geq
\operatorname{per}(A \circ \overline{A}),
$$

where $\operatorname{per}(A)$ is the permanent of a matrix $A$, and
$\circ$ denotes the Hadamard product.
